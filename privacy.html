Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

1. Overview

JARVIS is an AI assistant and automation platform designed to help users manage digital tasks, content, media and connected services through automated workflows.

This Privacy Policy explains what information JARVIS may process and how that information is used when users use JARVIS and its supported integrations.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features and integrations used, JARVIS may process information required to provide its services, including:

  • Account and authentication information.
  • Access tokens required for authorized integrations.
  • Media files selected by the user.
  • Content and metadata used in automated workflows.
  • Information required to communicate with connected third-party services.

3. Third-Party Services

JARVIS may integrate with third-party platforms and services, including social media, video and AI services.

For example, users may choose to connect services such as TikTok or YouTube to enable supported publishing and automation features.

JARVIS only accesses connected services when the user explicitly authorizes the applicable integration.

Third-party services have their own privacy policies, terms and data practices. Users should review the policies of services they choose to connect.

4. Use of Information

Information processed by JARVIS is used to provide the features requested by the user, including AI assistance, automation, content management, media workflows and authorized third-party integrations.

5. Data Storage

Where technically possible, authentication credentials, access tokens and related configuration information are intended to be stored locally by the JARVIS application.

Information is used only for the purposes necessary to provide features that the user has requested or authorized.

6. User Control

Users control which services they connect to and which content or media they choose to process or publish through JARVIS.

Users may discontinue an integration or stop using the associated JARVIS workflow at any time.

7. Data Sharing

JARVIS does not sell personal information.

Information may be transmitted to a third-party service when such transmission is necessary to perform an action explicitly requested or authorized by the user.

8. Security

JARVIS is designed to use reasonable measures to protect information used by the application. However, no software or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

JARVIS is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children who are not permitted to use connected third-party services.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when JARVIS features, integrations or privacy practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.

11. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or JARVIS, please use the contact information provided with the JARVIS application.
