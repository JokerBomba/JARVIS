Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

JARVIS AI is a desktop application designed to provide automation, media management and music publishing features.

Information We Process

JARVIS may process information required to provide its features, including authentication information, media files selected by the user, and information required to communicate with third-party services that the user explicitly connects to.

Third-Party Services

JARVIS may connect to third-party services such as YouTube and TikTok when the user explicitly authorizes those services. Their respective privacy policies and terms also apply to those services.

Data Storage

JARVIS does not sell personal information. Authentication credentials and tokens are intended to be stored locally by the application and are used only to provide the features authorized by the user.

User Control

Users control which third-party services they connect to and which media they choose to publish.

Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the JARVIS AI developer through the contact information provided with the application.
